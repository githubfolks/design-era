# Project: Convert Company Presentation PDF into a Premium Responsive Website

## Objective

You have been provided with a company presentation PDF.

Your task is to analyze the complete PDF and build a modern, premium, responsive business website that preserves the company's branding, messaging, visuals, and design language while significantly improving the user experience.

Do NOT simply recreate the slides.

Instead, transform the presentation into an enterprise-grade website.

---

## Requirements

Analyze every page of the PDF and extract:

- Company information
- Headlines
- Taglines
- Services
- Process
- Timeline
- Achievements
- Clients
- Certifications
- Images
- Icons
- Logos
- Colors

- Layout hierarchy

Use the extracted information to build a professional corporate website.
